# Code of Conduct

We want this to be a friendly project for everyone.

- Be respectful. No harassment, insults or discrimination.
- Assume good intent, and give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Keep discussion on topic and welcoming to beginners.

Maintainers may remove comments, issues or contributions that break these
rules, and may block repeat offenders. To report a problem, contact the
maintainer through the email on their GitHub profile.
